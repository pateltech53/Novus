## Intent

An Apple-keynote-style "Introducing Novus" launch film — v2, revised on user
feedback. The story: you can't learn to ride a bike by watching videos of
people riding bikes — yet business students sit through hours of lectures and
videos. Novus is the life simulator for running a company: months advance only
when you decide, every month throws a real business scenario at you, your
decisions move real numbers and the game remembers them, and the year only
closes when you pitch on camera to five AI sharks who have read your books.
Tagline: **LEARN by DOING.** Same visual world as v1: dark navy stage,
Liquid-Glass panes, enormous lowercase type, slow reverent reveals, real
captured UI as the evidence.
