# Novus — self-recorded walkthrough script

**Register:** Apple "Introducing". Calm, plain, unhurried. Short sentences. You are showing, not selling.
**Full version:** ~4:30. **Cutdowns at the end** (60s vertical, 30s, 15s hook).
**Rule for every line:** say it *while the thing is on screen*, then stop talking and let it breathe for a beat.

---

## Before you hit record

- [ ] **Fresh run.** Start from `/welcome` with no company (or clear the save) so founding is real, not replayed.
- [ ] **Window:** 1920×1080, browser in fullscreen, no bookmarks bar, no notifications, no dev "1 Issue" badge.
- [ ] **Zoom 100%.** Mac users: Cmd+0.
- [ ] **Mic:** headset or lav, 6–8" away, hard surface muffled. Record voice and screen on **separate tracks** if you can.
- [ ] **Two takes minimum** of the pitch section — you'll want the choice.
- [ ] **Decide the pitch content before recording.** You're going to speak on camera in-app; know your four beats cold (below).
- [ ] **Have a company name ready.** Use a real-sounding one — "Marrow & Co" reads better than "Test".
- [ ] **Do NOT say** "free", "just", "simply", "super easy", or any number the app didn't show you.

# THE FULL WALKTHROUGH

## 00 · Cold open — the hook (0:00–0:12)

**DO:** Black or your desktop with just the Novus landing page open. Don't move the cursor yet.

> **SAY:**
> "You don't learn to ride a bike by watching videos of people riding bikes.
> *(beat)*
> But that's how we teach business."

**DO:** Scroll the landing page once, slowly, to the shark. Stop.

> "So we built the other thing."

---

## 01 · Introducing (0:12–0:30)

**DO:** Land on the hero. Let the mascot sit there.

> **SAY:**
> "Introducing Novus — a life simulator for running a company.
> You make every call, month by month. And once a year, the game stops and asks you to defend it — out loud, on camera, to five investors who have read your numbers.
> Let's play a full year."

---

## 02 · Founding (0:30–1:00)

**DO:** Click through onboarding → land on `/found`. Type the company name **live** (don't paste — typing reads real). Pick an industry. Expand **WHAT THE COMPANY IS** and let the draft fill in.

> **SAY:**
> "You start with paperwork. Who's founding it. What it's called."
> *(type the name)*
> "What business you're in."
> *(pick industry)*
> "And what the company actually is — because the sharks will ask you all of this later, and what you write here stays on screen while you pitch."

**DO:** Hit **FOUND IT**.

> "That's it. You're in the garage, twenty-five thousand in the bank, month one of twelve."

---

## 03 · The play screen (1:00–1:35)

**DO:** Land on `/play`. **Don't click anything for three seconds.** Then move the cursor to each region as you name it.

> **SAY:**
> "This is your company on one screen.
> Your mascot, up top — he narrates, and he's not always nice.
> Three rings: brand, quality, morale. How the world sees you, how good the thing is, how your people feel.
> And the books: cash, burn, runway, valuation.
> *(beat)*
> Real words. Because they're real numbers — there are no coins in this game, and no XP."

**DO:** Hover the ADVANCE MONTH capsule without clicking.

> "And this. This is the only thing that moves time."

---

## 04 · The six tabs (1:35–2:15)

**DO:** Open each tab, ~4 seconds each, in order. Keep moving — this is a sweep, not a tour.

> **SAY, one line per tab as you open it:**
> - **COMPANY** — "Your dossier. Everything the sharks can look up."
> - **TEAM** — "Hiring and firing. Salaries come out of the same cash you're watching."
> - **PRODUCT** — "What you actually sell, and what it costs you to make it."
> - **ASSETS** — "What you own. What you could sell when it gets tight."
> - **MARKET** — "And this is your phone." *(let it rise)* "Your founder money is separate from company money — you can invest it, or you can lose it."
> - **CLOSET** — "And the closet, which is cosmetic. Nothing in here changes your score, your survival, or the leaderboard."

---

## 05 · Advancing a month (2:15–2:40)

**DO:** Back to the play screen. Press **ADVANCE MONTH**. Let the month turn and the card land. Don't narrate over the animation.

> **SAY (after the card lands):**
> "Press advance, and the month happens. Revenue comes in, burn goes out —
> and something lands on your desk."

---

## 06 · A decision (2:40–3:20)

**DO:** Read the card on screen for a beat. Hover each choice so the tradeoffs show. Commit one. **Pick the option that costs you something** — it makes the next section honest.

> **SAY:**
> "Every month brings a real scenario. Read it. Every choice tells you what it costs before you take it —
> *(hover)* this one buys you margin and hurts your brand. This one protects the brand and eats cash.
> *(commit)*
> You decide. Then you live with it."

**DO:** Let the books update behind the card. Point at the deltas.

> "And the game remembers. Cash moved. Runway moved. By month twelve, these numbers *are* your story."

---

## 07 · The year (3:20–3:40)

**DO:** Advance months quickly — 3–4 in a row, committing fast. Let one interesting event play. Then hit month 12 so the capsule turns gold.

> **SAY (over the fast months):**
> "Twelve months of this. Suppliers squeeze. Rivals copy. A critic writes something.
> *(the capsule turns gold)*
> And then the year stops."

---

## 08 · The gate (3:40–4:00)

**DO:** Press **CLOSE THE YEAR**. Land on the "Pitch me" brief. Let the four beats sit on screen.

> **SAY:**
> "You can't close the year from a menu. To close it, you pitch.
> Sixty seconds: what you sell, who buys it, why you win, and what you want from them.
> *(beat)*
> Camera on."

---

## 09 · The pitch — YOU ACTUALLY PITCH (4:00–4:50)

**DO:** Press **OPEN THE CAMERA**. Let the permission and self-view land. Press **START TALKING** and *genuinely pitch your company for ~30–40 seconds.* Use your real numbers off the screen.

> **SAY (your own words — this is the point of the video). A workable shape:**
> "We're [company]. We sell [product] to [customer].
> We're doing [revenue] a year at [margin], burning [burn] a month, with [runway] months of runway.
> We win because [the one real reason].
> I'm asking for [amount] for [equity], and it buys me [what it buys]."

**DO:** When it stops, **don't cut**. Let the coach line and the score land on screen.

> **SAY (after):**
> "It's listening to the words and the numbers — the structure you covered, whether the figures match your own books, your filler words.
> And if your mic fails or you'd rather type it, there's a text path that scores exactly the same."

---

## 10 · The Tank (4:50–5:40)

**DO:** Enter the panel. Let the Chair read your ask into the record. Press **LET THEM ASK**. Answer at least one question out loud. Then let the offers land.

> **SAY:**
> "Five sharks. They've read your books.
> *(question lands)* They ask, you answer — out loud, or typed.
> *(offers land)* And then they decide."

**DO:** Hover the offers so the math shows.

> "Every offer shows its math: what they're putting in, what they want for it, and what that values you at.
> You can take one. You can take none."

**DO:** Sign one. Let the score card land and hold it.

> "And then you find out how you actually did."

---

## 11 · The verdict, and year two (5:40–6:10)

**DO:** Walk the debrief → the year-end statement → the allocation choice → year two opening.

> **SAY:**
> "A grade on the pitch, a grade on the deal, and exactly what was missing.
> Your year, closed on the record.
> You choose where next year's money goes —
> *(year 2 opens)* and year two starts, with the deal on your books and the dilution on your cap table.
> *(beat)*
> Survive, and it gets harder. Don't, and Chapter Seven is real — the game will show you which decisions actually killed you."

---

## 12 · Close (6:10–6:25)

**DO:** Back to a clean play screen, or the lockup.

> **SAY:**
> "That's Novus. Your company, your calls, your pitch.
> Learn by doing.
> novuspitch.com."

# Things worth saying if you have room

Drop any of these where they fit — each is true and each answers a real question:

- **"After three years, the pitch becomes optional."** — "The first three years, you pitch. After that the gate lets you close quietly if you want — it just doesn't get you a deal."
- **Rookie Mode** — "If burn rate and runway are new words, Rookie Mode adds the plain-English line next to the real term. It never replaces it."
- **No account needed** — "You can play without an account. Make one and your companies follow you between devices."
- **The camera is real, the fallback is real** — "Every camera moment has a typed path, scored on content only."
- **Honesty line** — "Nothing you can buy touches your score, your survival, or the leaderboard."

# CUTDOWNS

## 60-second vertical (9:16, for Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

Record these six shots, 8–12s each, in this order:

1. **Hook** — landing page. *"You don't learn to ride a bike by watching videos of people riding bikes. That's how we teach business."*
2. **The screen** — play screen. *"This is your company. Cash, burn, runway. Real numbers."*
3. **The press** — ADVANCE MONTH, the month turns, a card lands. *"Time only moves when you move it."*
4. **The call** — commit a choice. *"Every choice costs something. The game remembers."*
5. **The gate** — capsule goes gold → "Pitch me". *"You can't close the year from a menu. You pitch it. On camera."*
6. **The Tank** — the five sharks + one offer. *"To five investors who read your books."* → cut to **"LEARN BY DOING · novuspitch.com"**

## 30-second

Shots 1 → 3 → 5 → 6. Same lines, no pauses between.

## 15-second hooks (pick one, record all three)

- *"A business sim where you have to actually pitch — out loud, on camera — or the year doesn't end."*
- *"I ran a company for twelve months, then five AI sharks read my books and asked me why I'm worth it."*
- *"You can't learn business by watching. So this one makes you talk."*

# Recording notes that will save you a re-shoot

- **Silence is the style.** Every time you finish a sentence, stop for one full second before clicking. You can tighten in the edit; you can't add air.
- **Never narrate an animation while it's moving.** Let the month turn, *then* speak.
- **Cursor discipline.** Move it deliberately to what you're naming, then park it. No idle circling.
- **The pitch section is the video.** If it's flat, the whole thing is flat — do it three times and keep the one where you sound like you mean it.
- **Don't apologize on camera** ("um, sorry, let me..."). Just stop, breathe, and restart the line — you'll cut it.
- **Capture 5 seconds of room tone** before you start, so your editor can patch gaps cleanly.
