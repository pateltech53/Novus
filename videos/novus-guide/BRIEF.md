## Intent

A separate full app guide: how Novus works, walked through one complete company
life — founding, the play screen, advancing months, decisions and their
consequences, the year gate, the on-camera pitch to the sharks, and the
verdict. Show-it-as-is: real captured screens and clips are the featured
assets, presented large and legible. LOOK IS INVERTED from the launch film:
**white/warm-paper background, dark ink** (the app's own light theme), same
Apple smoothness — slow pushes, restrained reveals, calm narration.
