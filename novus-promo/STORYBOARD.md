# Novus B2C Promo — v2 storyboard (84s · 1920×1080 · 30fps · 120 BPM, 2s bars)

Every scene boundary lands on a bar line. The current is LEFT; reserved vectors:
Z-push = deeper into the product, Z-pull = arrival (mid-hook, CTA), UP once (out of
the tank). Full seam plan: `ledger.json`. Music: `assets/music.mp3` (synthesized in
`scripts/make-music.mjs`; sections keyed to these windows).

Worlds: LIGHT (warm paper `#F7F5F0`, navy text) is the default; DARK (`#0B1E36`)
is spent on the reveal and the tank act only.
